PROBLEM-SOLVING FRAMEWORK

PREVENT • REDUCE • REPAIR

Use completed pupil evidence before moving to the rescue mission.
🛑
PREVENT
Stop the environmental problem before it happens whenever possible.
Preferred when practical because damage is avoided before it begins.
⚠️
REDUCE
If an activity must continue, minimise the harmful activity or impact as much as possible.
Use safer methods, smaller amounts or better controls.
🔧
REPAIR
When damage has already happened, restore and fix the environment.
Do not play the blaming game—repair the damage.
Understanding the damage is not enough. We must choose an action that really solves the problem.
