Teacher Explanation • 9.2.1 + 9.2.2

What we see + How rocks form

IGNEOUS

Characteristic: Contains various minerals.

Formation: Magma/lava → cooling and freezing → igneous rock.

SEDIMENTARY

Characteristics: Many layers; sometimes hollow; may contain fossils.

Formation: Deposited/eroded materials → compression → sedimentary rock.

METAMORPHIC

Characteristic: Usually harder than the original rock.

Formation: Existing rock → very high heat + pressure → metamorphic rock.
