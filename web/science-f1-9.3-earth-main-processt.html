04 / Compare · Think aloud

Surface or inside Earth?

The key question is where the process happens or is driven from.

⏱ 5 min
OUTSIDE / SURFACE

Exogenic

Processes that happen on Earth's surface.

  • Weathering
  • Erosion
  • Mass and land depletion
  • Transport and sedimentation
Think: rain, water, waves, wind, gravity.
INSIDE / INTERNAL FORCE

Endogenic

Processes caused by forces inside Earth.

  • Mantle convection
  • Earth's crust movement
  • Magma activity
Think: mantle, crustal plates, magma.
💬 A cliff is worn away by sea waves. Which process is this, and why?
"It is exogenic because waves wear away rock at Earth's surface. This is erosion."

60-minute lesson guide

Teacher-led, highly interactive. Cover the basics of 9.3 only.

  1. 5 min — Hook: Predict differences between coastal erosion and magma activity.
  2. 14 min — Exogenic: Explore weathering, erosion, mass/land depletion and transport/sedimentation.
  3. 14 min — Endogenic: Show mantle convection, crust movement, magma activity.
  4. 5 min — Compare: Surface processes versus forces from inside Earth.
  5. 10 min — Sort: Select 2–3 volunteers; involve all students in every decision.
  6. 9 min — Quiz: Whole class votes with fingers before teacher taps.
  7. 3 min — Exit: Return to original pictures; ask for scientific explanations.

Target: Identify both categories, give examples, and classify at least five out of six examples.

Language: Main instruction in simple English, with Malay or Mandarin supporting terms from the top menu. Use gestures, visuals and sentence frames.

Smartboard support: Use fullscreen for demonstration. Drag/drop also supports tap-to-place. No internet, accounts or installation required.

Important: These are simplified teaching diagrams, not accurate geological scales or measured predictions.
